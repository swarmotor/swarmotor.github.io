研究焦点Focus Areas

01 具身数据生成 Embodied Data Generation

研究 R2S2R、仿真轨迹合成与自动数据生成，构建覆盖任务变化和失败状态的数据。

Studying R2S2R, simulated trajectory synthesis, and automatic generation for task variation and failure-state coverage.

02 数据质量与评测 Data Quality & Evaluation

结合 VLM 语义判断、WAM 动力学预测与仿真真值，诊断 rollout 质量和失败原因。

Combining VLM semantic judgment, WAM dynamics prediction, and simulator ground truth to diagnose rollout quality and failures.

03 数据驱动后训练 Data-Driven Post-Training

以 VLA 为策略载体，围绕数据契约、微调、成对 rollout 与反馈回流组织可复现闭环。

Using VLAs as policy models in reproducible loops spanning data contracts, fine-tuning, paired rollouts, and feedback collection.

04 群体机器人方法 Swarm Robotics Methods

以拓扑保持感知、图论、分布式优化和稳定性分析作为多机器人数据与策略研究基础。

Using topology-preserving perception, graph theory, distributed optimization, and stability analysis as foundations for multi-robot data and policy research.
